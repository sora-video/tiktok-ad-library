# TikTok Ad Library

*Unofficial community guide for the TikTok Ad Library. Not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance. All trademarks belong to their owners.*

The tiktok ad library is TikTok's public Commercial Content Library at library.tiktok.com: a searchable record of ads, an all-ads report, a section for other commercial content, and an FAQ. It sits next to a second tool that people often mean when they use the same words - Top Ads inside TikTok Creative Center, which shows high-performing auction ads with filters for objective, region, likes and duration. This guide covers both, how to get into each, what they can and cannot tell you about a competitor, and where the creative-side tools around them fit.

## What it is

The Commercial Content Library is TikTok's transparency surface. Its navigation is small and tells you what it is for: an Ad Library, an All ads report, an Other commercial content section, and FAQs, with a language selector (English (US) by default) and links to the Help Center, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. It exists so that anyone can see what commercial content has run on the platform, which is a different purpose from helping advertisers find inspiration.

Top Ads in TikTok Creative Center is the inspiration tool. Its own description is that you can see high-performing auction ads on TikTok and let successful cases inspire your creativity. The page offers filters for Objective, Region, Sort by, Likes and Duration, plus a keyword search, and shows a No search results found state until you set them. Creative Center wraps Top Ads with Trends, Creative Tools (Creative Studio, Automation and an API section), and an Influencer marketing entry that leads to TikTok One, the creator marketplace. It shows Log in and Sign up controls, so expect an account for the full experience.

## How to get started

1. Decide which question you are asking. Compliance or research about what has run: start at the Commercial Content Library. Creative inspiration or competitor benchmarking by performance: start at Creative Center Top Ads.
2. For the library, open library.tiktok.com/ads, read the FAQs page once so you know what the library does and does not include, then search. Use the All ads report if you want the aggregate view rather than individual ads.
3. For Top Ads, open the Creative Center page, set Region first (results are region-scoped), then Objective, then sort. The page renders nothing until filters are set, so an empty page on arrival is normal.
4. Use the keyword search on Top Ads to narrow to a product category or a competitor's brand terms.
5. Save what you find outside the tool - a spreadsheet with the ad, region, objective, and the date you saw it - because neither page is designed as your archive.
6. If you want to build creative from what you found, Creative Center's Creative Studio (Symphony) covers text to video, image to video, avatar videos, product remixes and translation and dubbing into 15 languages.

## Pricing and limits

Neither the Commercial Content Library page nor the Top Ads page shows a price. Creative Center asks you to log in or sign up, so treat some of it as gated behind a TikTok for Business account. Rate limits and export options are not documented on either page; the Creative Center API section exists but its details are not in the pages this guide is based on, so check it directly before planning any automated pull.

## Practical notes and gotchas

- **Two tools, two purposes.** The library is transparency; Top Ads is performance-ranked inspiration. Searching a competitor in the library shows what they ran; searching Top Ads shows only the ads that performed well enough to surface.
- **Region scopes everything on Top Ads.** Set it before anything else, and re-run the same query per market when you are comparing.
- **The empty state is not an error.** Top Ads shows No search results found until filters are applied. Do not assume the tool is broken.
- **Read the policies pages once.** Creative Center links its Guidelines, Ad Policies and Ad Specifications. If you are going to reproduce a format you saw in the library, the specifications page is the one that tells you what will actually be accepted.
- **There is no built-in alerting.** Both pages are point-in-time views. If you need to know when a competitor launches something new, you have to come back and look, or use a monitoring tool.
- **Creators are a separate surface.** Influencer discovery lives under TikTok One / the creator marketplace, not in the ad library. If your research is really about who is making the ads, plan for that as a second step.

## FAQ

**Is the tiktok ad library the same as Creative Center Top Ads?**
No. The library at library.tiktok.com is a transparency record with an all-ads report and FAQs; Top Ads is a Creative Center feature that ranks high-performing auction ads for inspiration. People use the phrase for both.

**Do I need an account?**
The Commercial Content Library landing page shows no login. Creative Center shows Log in and Sign up controls, so expect to need a TikTok for Business account for its tools.

**Why does Top Ads show nothing when I open it?**
It renders results only after you set filters. Choose a Region and Objective and the list fills in.

**Can I get the data through an API?**
Creative Center has an API section under Creative Tools, but its scope and terms are not described on the pages this guide draws from. Look at that section and the developer site (developers.tiktok.com) before assuming anything.

**Where do I find the creators behind the ads?**
Not in the library. Creative Center's Influencer marketing entry points to TikTok One, the creator marketplace; a monitoring tool that does both ads and creators is the shortcut.
